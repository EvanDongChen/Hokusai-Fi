    The studio

    Every block is carved by code.

    Hokusai-Fi pulls woodblock prints in the manner of Hokusai's Under the Wave off Kanagawa, with no photographs and no tracing. A handful of curves place the great wave, the swells, the boats and Fuji; carving procedures do the rest: fingers of blue rising into foam, talons that curl over with a dark hook beneath them, strands of lighter blue running through the water, flecks of spray. Tune to 1831 and the composition is Hokusai's own. Every other station is an edition of its own, with its own swell, weather and crew.

    The radio plays a lo-fi beat composed live from the same seed: koto, shakuhachi, warm keys, a temple bell and the sea. Set sail and the print opens out into an endless sea, printed just ahead of you, while the music turns with the weather.
