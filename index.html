About this sea

Every line is code.

Hokusai-Fi prints Hokusai's Under the Wave off Kanagawa from nothing but code: no photos, no AI, no training data. Like a woodblock print it is built up one block at a time: the graded sky, the sea, each wave's body, the light bands carved along it, the key-block outline, and the foam, whose claws split into curling talons and throw spray across the sky.

Gallery hangs the 1831 composition, reinterpreted for every seed with its own weather. Wander steps into the print and keeps rowing, past the Kanagawa sea, open swells with fishing boats, a calm bay under a red Fuji, pine-covered headlands and rocky islets, printed just ahead of you. Turn on life and the spray flies, the birds cross and the snow falls; turn on the sound for a lo-fi beat with koto and shakuhachi that changes with the weather.
